# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Ombelle UI to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code review.
- Assume good faith and give people room to learn.
- Focus on the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or slurs, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, and spam.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate. They will explain the decision when it is safe to do so.

## Reporting

Report a violation privately through GitHub: [open a private report](https://github.com/whritou/ombelle-ui/security/advisories/new) and start the title with "Conduct". Reports are handled by the maintainers and kept confidential. If the report concerns a maintainer, say so and it will be handled by another one where possible.

## Scope and attribution

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
